Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Application: Bharat GST Free Billing (Created by Abhishek Agrahari)
Effective Date: 21 November 2026

1. Zero Server Collection (No Data Fiduciary Cloud Storage)

Bharat GST Free Billing operates strictly as a client-side / local-device application. We do not run any backend database, cloud accounting server, telemetry endpoint, or advertising network that receives your business records.

  • Stored strictly on your device: Invoices, quotations, credit/debit notes, client directories, GSTINs, PANs, HSN/SAC items, inventory counts, expenses, bank account details, UPI IDs, logos, signatures, and print settings are stored exclusively in your browser’s local IndexedDB database (bharat_gst_billing_idb) or your local desktop data folder.
  • Zero analytics & zero trackers: No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, behavioral tracking cookies, or fingerprinting scripts are loaded.

2. Local Encryption & Security Controls

  • AES-256-GCM Data Vault: When you enable the Encrypted Data Vault in Settings → Privacy & Security, sensitive business records and .fgsb backup archives are encrypted locally using WebCrypto PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (310,000 iterations) and AES-256-GCM. Your passphrase never leaves your device and cannot be recovered by anyone else.
  • Role-Based Staff PINs: Admin and Staff PINs are stored only as cryptographic verification hashes on your device.
  • Redacted Diagnostics: Local problem logs automatically scrub GSTIN, PAN, phone numbers, email addresses, UPI IDs, and bank account numbers before displaying diagnostic text.

3. Optional Third-Party Connections Initiated Only by You

  • Google Drive Backup (Optional): Only if you explicitly connect your own Google account in Settings, your browser communicates directly with www.googleapis.com and accounts.google.com using the narrow drive.file scope so backups are saved into your own Google Drive.
  • WhatsApp Sharing (Optional): Clicking Share on WhatsApp opens a wa.me link in your browser or WhatsApp app only when you click it.

4. Your Rights & Data Portability (India DPDP Act, 2023)

Because all records reside directly on your device under your sole custody, you have immediate, unrestricted control to inspect, edit, export (JSON, CSV, Excel-ready, PDF, GST Portal JSON, or encrypted .fgsb), or permanently erase your data at any time via Settings → Backup & Restore.
