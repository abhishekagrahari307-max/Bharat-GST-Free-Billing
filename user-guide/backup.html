Backups, updates and moving PC

Your data is plain files on your own computer. Here is how it is backed up, how to keep a copy somewhere safe, update the app, and move to a new computer.

#Where your data is

Everything is in the _system\data folder inside the app's folder. The installer hides _system to keep things tidy; in File Explorer, turn on View, Show, Hidden items to see it. Open Data Folder under Backup files (ZIP) on the Data & Backups tab of Settings opens it for you.

Downloaded PDFs are also kept in the app's Saved Invoices folder, one folder per client.

#Daily backups (Backup Management)

Every day the app copies all your data, invoices, clients, products, purchases, expenses, settings and the trash, into a dated backup, and keeps the last 30 days. The first backup of the day is made a few seconds after the app starts, and a computer left on overnight makes the next one soon after midnight. You do not have to do anything.

They are on the Backups tab of Settings, in the 💾 Backup Management + 🗑 Trash Bin card:

Part of the cardWhat it does
Backup nowMakes today's backup straight away, for example before a big change. The message says Manual backup triggered.
Also keep a copy of every daily backup in this folderA second copy on another drive. See Keeping a copy somewhere else.
📅 Daily backupsThe list of backups, newest first, with Restore and Delete. See Restoring a daily backup.
🗑 Trash binDeleted invoices, kept for 30 days. See Trash bin.
These are on the same computer

Daily backups protect you from mistakes, not from a broken hard disk or a lost laptop. Keep a copy somewhere else too.

#Restoring a daily backup

Each row of 📅 Daily backups shows the date and time it was made, and also in your backup folder when a second copy exists. Use Restore when something went badly wrong, for example many records deleted by mistake, and you want all your data back as it was on that day.

  1. Pick the dayFind the last day your data was right and click Restore on its row.
  2. ConfirmThe app asks Restore all data from backup …? and explains that it overwrites your current data. Click Restore backup.
  3. ReloadWhen it says Backup restored — please reload the page, press F5.

Before restoring, the app saves your data as it is now in data/backups/pre-restore-…, so you can go back if the restore looks wrong.

Delete removes one backup after asking, to free space; the copy in your backup folder stays. A row marked in your backup folder only has no Delete button.

Only one invoice is wrong?

Restoring a day puts everything back to that day, including work done since. For one deleted invoice, use the Trash bin instead.

#Backup files (ZIP)

On the Data & Backups tab of Settings, right under the daily backups, the Backup files (ZIP) card saves your whole data folder as one ZIP file, to keep on a pen drive or carry to a new computer. (These buttons were on the Control Panel screen until version 1.10.86.)

CardButtonWhat it does
Backup DataCreate BackupSaves your data as a dated ZIP in Documents\BharatGST Backups.
Restore BackupChoose Backup ZIPPick a backup ZIP to restore. What you have now is saved first, as a safety net.
Move to Another PCExport for MoveSaves your data and settings as one ZIP on your Desktop. See Moving to a new computer.
Open Data FolderOpenOpens the folder with your bills, clients, products and settings.

Three kinds of backup, three buttons: Backup now in the daily backups card above makes today's daily backup inside the app's data folder; Create Backup here makes a ZIP outside it; Export Backup saves a .json file with the parts you choose. After each button here a panel shows whether it worked: see The result panel.

#Backup Data

What it does. Saves all your bills, clients, products and settings as one dated ZIP file, backup-date_time.zip, in the Documents\BharatGST Backups folder (Mac and Linux: Documents/BharatGST Backups).

When to use it. Before a big change, at the end of the month, or any time you want a copy to keep on a pen drive. The app also makes its own daily backup; this is an extra one at a moment you choose.

  1. Click Create Backup. It shows Backing up... for a moment.
  2. The result panel below says Backup - OK, with the file name and size in its output. A folder window usually opens with the new file shown.
  3. Copy that ZIP to a pen drive or cloud storage so it survives if this computer breaks. Open Backups Folder, on the same card, opens the folder.

Is it safe? Yes. It only reads your data and writes a new file. Old backups are never replaced.

Something wrong? If it says "No data folder found - nothing to back up", or fails with a disk or permission message, see a backup or restore failed, the disk is full and the app is not allowed to write. More ways to back up: Backups and updates.

#Restore Backup

What it does. Replaces the data in the app with the data from a backup ZIP you choose.

When to use it. After moving to a new computer, or to go back to how things were on an earlier day. It works with ZIPs made by Create Backup or Export for Move.

  1. Windows. Click Choose Backup ZIP. A file window opens in your BharatGST Backups folder. Pick the ZIP and click Open. (Close the window to cancel; nothing changes.)
  2. The app first saves what you have now as pre-restore-date.zip in the same folder. This is your way back if you chose the wrong file.
  3. It then replaces the data with the backup's contents and shows [OK] Restore complete! Press Enter to close the black window.
  4. Restart the app (Restart the App) to see the restored data.

Mac and Linux: this button says Not available here. To bring data back there, use Settings, Backup Management, Import Backup with a file saved by Export Backup (see Export and import).

Is it safe? Your current data is replaced by the backup, so what you entered after that backup was made will not be there. That is why a pre-restore copy is saved first. Keep it until you are happy.

Something wrong? See a backup or restore failed. If the old data still shows, restart the app; if it will not open, see the server has not answered.

#Move to Another PC

From v1.10.86 there is no separate card: use Create Backup on the old computer and Restore Backup on the new one; see Moving to a new computer. The launcher (ZIP install on Windows) still has More options → Move to another PC, which does the following.

What it does. Saves your data as one ZIP on your Desktop named MOVE-BharatGST-date_time.zip, ready to carry to a new computer.

When to use it. When you buy a new computer, or want the same books on a second one.

  1. Windows: in the launcher, More options → Move to another PC. A black window shows the progress and the size, then a folder window opens with the ZIP highlighted on your Desktop.
  2. Copy the ZIP to a pen drive, or send it to yourself by email or cloud storage.
  3. On the new PC, install the app (Install guide), open Settings → Data & Backups and use Choose Backup ZIP (Restore Backup) to pick this file.

Mac and Linux: follow Moving to a new computer.

Is it safe? Yes. It only makes a copy; your data here stays as it is. Keep the ZIP private, since it holds your business records.

Something wrong? If it says "No data folder to export", or the disk is full, see the disk is full; for the restore step see a backup or restore failed.

#Keeping a copy somewhere else

Every day, automatically, on another drive. On the Backups tab of Settings, under Also keep a copy of every daily backup in this folder:

  1. Type a folderOn another drive, a USB disk or a synced folder, such as D:\BharatGST or E:\.
  2. Save itClick Save folder. The app checks it can write there and copies today's backup at once. The message says where the copies go.

Each daily backup is then also copied into BharatGST Backups\date inside that folder, keeping 30 days. The line under the box shows where. If a copy fails (a USB disk not plugged in), the card says so in red. Stop copying turns it off; copies already made stay.

If the computer fails: install the app again, set the same folder here, and its backups appear in the list with Restore.

Or by hand:

  • One click: Create Backup under Backup files (ZIP), or Back up my data in the launcher under More options, saves a ZIP in Documents\BharatGST Backups. Copy it to a pen drive or cloud storage.
  • One file you choose: see Export Backup.
  • Google Drive: see Cloud Backup (Google Drive).

Something wrong?

  • "Could not use that folder": the folder does not exist, or the app may not write there. Make the folder first in File Explorer, or pick another drive.
  • "copying today's backup failed": the folder was saved, but the drive was not ready. Plug the disk in; the next daily backup tries again.

#Export Backup and Import Backup (Data Management)

On the Import/Export tab of Settings, the Data Management card saves your data to one file, or brings a file back. Use it to keep a copy on a pen drive, to move data to another computer, or to bring back just your clients or products from an older backup.

Your data is on this computer only. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else; the only time anything leaves your computer is when you tick Also save a copy to my Google Drive, which goes to your own Drive. The file is plain text (JSON) you can keep anywhere.

  • Export Backup…: see Export Backup.
  • Import Backup…: see Restore from Backup.

#Export Backup

  2. Choose what to includeUntick anything you do not want. Select all and Clear all tick or untick every one.

  4. DownloadClick Download Backup. The file bharat-gst-backup-date.json goes to your Downloads folder. Copy it somewhere safe.

#Restore from Backup

  1. Pick the fileClick Import Backup… and choose a .json backup file.
  2. Check what is in itThe Restore from Backup window shows when the file was made and, for each part, how many items it holds. Parts the file has are ticked; empty parts say empty and cannot be ticked.
  3. Choose what to restoreUntick anything you want to keep as it is now. Select all (with data) and Clear all help.
  4. RestoreClick Restore selected. The message lists what came back, such as Restored: 120 invoice(s), 45 client(s).

Records in the file replace the matching records in the app; records the file does not have are left alone, and parts you did not tick are untouched. Export your current data first, just in case.

Something wrong?

  • "This file doesn't look like a Bharat GST Free Billing backup": you picked a different file. Backups are named bharat-gst-backup-date.json. A backup ZIP is restored with Choose Backup ZIP under Backup files (ZIP).
  • Restored, but I do not see it: reload the page, and check the business selected at the top of the sidebar.

#Trash bin

Invoices deleted from a client's account on the Clients screen, or from the invoice list, go to the trash for 30 days, then are removed for good. They are listed under 🗑 Trash bin on the Backups tab of Settings, with the invoice number, client and date deleted.

  • Restore puts the invoice back where it was. The stock its delete returned is taken out again. The message says Invoice restored.
  • Delete forever removes it and its PDF straight away, after asking. This cannot be undone.

For an invoice already given to a client, cancel it instead of deleting it, so its number stays in your records.

#Close the books

After you file a month's GST returns, close the books up to its last day, so nothing in a filed month changes by mistake. In Settings, on the Data & Backups tab, under Close the books, pick the last day (for example 31 October 2026) and click Close the books.

  • Invoices, purchase bills, expenses, debit notes, stock adjustments and cash book entries dated on or before that day can no longer be added, deleted, or changed in their date, number, items, amounts, GST (including SEZ, export and reverse charge) or cancellation. The message says why, for example the books are closed up to 2026-10-31.
  • What does not change the accounts still works: recording a payment or a receipt against an old invoice or purchase bill, printing, sharing and notes.
  • With the App lock on, closing or opening the books asks for its PIN. It is done on the PC itself, not from a paired phone.
  • A deleted invoice dated in a closed month cannot be restored from the Trash until the books are opened.
  • To correct a filed month, Open the books, make the change (your GST return may need an amendment: ask your CA), and close them again. Closing and opening are written in the edit history.
  • The server checks every save, so a phone or tablet cannot change a closed month either. A restore from a backup or an import keeps the books closed (or open) as they are now, and keeps this PC's edit history; the restore itself is written in it.

#Edit history

Under Close the books, Show the edit history lists the latest changes, newest first: when, what (an invoice added, a purchase bill changed, an expense deleted...), its number, its date, and the amount (before and after when it changed). A change made from a phone says (phone). Pick one kind in the list to see only those. The history starts with this version: earlier changes are not in it. It is kept in your books folder (data/edit-history, one file a month) and copied with the automatic backups.

#Moving to a new computer

  1. On the old computerClick Create Backup under Settings → Data & Backups → Backup files (ZIP), then Open Backups Folder. The newest ZIP holds your data and settings. Copy it to a pen drive or cloud storage.
  2. On the new computerInstall the app as usual.
  3. Bring your data inIn the new copy, open Settings → Data & Backups, click Choose Backup ZIP under Restore Backup and pick the file.

You can use an exported backup file instead: export on the old computer, and import it in Settings on the new one.

One computer at a time

Do not run the same data on two computers at once, for example through a shared OneDrive or Dropbox folder. Two copies writing the same files can damage them.

#Updating

Any of these installs the newest version:

  • The Update to v… button that appears in the sidebar when a new version is out. It shows what changed, with Update Now, Remind me later and Skip this version.
  • Update Now in Settings → App & updates.
  • In the launcher, More options, then Update to the latest version.

Every update first saves a backup of your data in Documents\BharatGST Backups, then replaces the app. It never touches your data folder or your saved PDFs. It takes about a minute: a window shows each step, and the page reloads on the new version by itself.

To update by hand instead: download the ZIP, stop the app, extract the ZIP over your app folder replacing the files, and open the launcher again.

If you extract a new ZIP into a different folder by mistake, its launcher does not install a second copy. It shows Already installed on this computer, with the folder, version and number of invoices, and offers Update the installed copy and Open the installed copy. The update uses the files in the folder you extracted, so it needs no download and works without internet; your invoices, saved PDFs and trash stay as they are. The new folder can then be deleted.

Updating also removes files that older versions left behind, such as launchers under their old names, once their replacements are in place. Your data, backups and Node.js are never touched.
