#Complete Feature Suite (v2.6.0 — Daily Auto-Update Edition)

Bharat GST Free Billing (v2.6.0) — Created by Abhishek Agrahari — is a complete offline-first, AES-256-GCM encrypted GST invoicing, AI voice billing, inventory, accounting, and tax return platform for Indian businesses and international exporters (23 countries, 22 currencies).

  • 🎙️ Bharat AI Voice & Fast Command Billing: Speak or type naturally in Hinglish, Hindi (हिंदी), or English inside New Invoice to auto-match saved Clients, Products, HSN/SAC codes, Rates, Units, and GST slabs 100% offline.
  • All 10 GST & Business Document Types: Tax Invoice, Proforma Invoice, Quotation / Estimate, Credit Note, Debit Note, Bill of Supply, Composition Bill, Sales Order, Purchase Order, and Delivery Challan — with automatic CGST+SGST, IGST, UTGST, Cess, Reverse Charge (RCM), SEZ & LUT Export handling.
  • 🩺 Pre-Filing CA GST Health Auditor + Full GST Suite: 0–100% GST Portal Readiness Score, portal-ready GSTR-1 JSON & CSV, GSTR-3B JSON & CSV, GSTR-2B ITC Reconciliation, e-Invoice IRP JSON & QR, e-Way Bill JSON, TDS / TCS (Form 26Q / 27EQ), Income Tax Calculator (Old vs New Regime, 87A, 234B/234C), and Period Books Lock after filing.
  • 9 Specialized Indian Industry Presets: Pharmacy & Medical (Batch, Expiry, Drug Licence), Mobile & Electronics (IMEI/Serial, Warranty), Jewellery (Purity, Gross/Net Wt, Making Charges), Retail/Kirana POS, Transport (LR/Vehicle), Textiles, Auto Parts, Hardware, and Freelance/IT.
  • 5 Print Layouts, 8 Colour Designs, 5 Bill Languages & Thermal POS: Print on A4, A5, Letter, or 58mm / 80mm thermal receipt printers with headings in English, Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, or Bengali, Dark Mode, dynamic UPI QR codes, logo, stamp, and signature.
  • Complete Stock, Barcode, OCR & Migration Tools: Multi-unit conversion (1 Box = 12 Pcs), barcode scanner & label printing, serial/IMEI tracking, low-stock alerts, Manufacturing BOM, Purchase Bills with offline Tesseract photo OCR, and 1-click import from Vyapar, Tally XML, Excel/CSV, and Flipkart orders.
  • Full Reports, Cash Book & Bank Reconciliation: Profit & Loss, Party-wise Outstanding & Aging, Client & Supplier PDF Statements, Stock Valuation, Receipt Vouchers, Cash Book, Expense Tracker, Cost Centres, and Bank Statement CSV/XLSX matching.
  • AES-256-GCM Security, Multi-Business & Daily Auto-Updates: WebCrypto AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2 (310,000 rounds) Vault, Fingerprint/Passkey unlock, Multi-Staff Role PINs, Per-Business PIN lock, Edit History audit trail, Trash Bin recovery, Same-Wi-Fi Phone QR Pairing, Google Drive auto-backup, and Daily Continuous Safe Auto-Updates.

#What you need

ComputerWindows 10 or 11, macOS, or Linux (a NAS works too).
BrowserChrome, Edge, Firefox or Brave. The app opens in it.
Disk spaceRoughly 200 MB once installed.
InternetOnly for the first install and for updates. Everything else works offline.
Account or signupNone.

#Where your data lives

Every invoice, client and setting is saved as plain files inside the app's folder, in _system\data. They never leave your computer unless you turn on Google Drive backup yourself.

That also means backing up is your job, and it is easy. See Backups, updates and moving PC.

#Is it really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT licence. There is no trial, no invoice limit, no feature that unlocks for money, and no advertising. The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does. Is it safe? See Security and privacy.

#Getting help

Every screen in the app with a ? button links to the matching part of these pages. If something is missing here, or the app does something these pages don't describe, open an issue on GitHub with a screenshot. Many features began as someone's request.
