Privacy Notice & Data Protection (DPDP)

How Bharat GST Free Billing (Created by Abhishek Agrahari) handles business, client, and invoice data under Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) principles.

#Zero cloud collection — 100% on your device

Bharat GST Free Billing is a local-first web and desktop application. We do not operate a central cloud database, do not require user registration, and do not collect, transmit, sell, or profile your invoices, GSTINs, client names, phone numbers, bank accounts, or tax returns.

  • Where your data lives: All business profiles, invoices, clients, inventory, purchases, expenses, and settings are stored strictly inside your own browser's IndexedDB (or your local computer's data/ folder when running the local desktop server).
  • Encryption at rest: When you enable the PIN Lock / Encrypted Vault in Settings → Security, sensitive records are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with keys derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (310,000 iterations).
  • Zero analytics or ad trackers: The application embeds zero third-party analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or external font CDNs.

#Optional user-initiated connections

Network requests happen only when you explicitly trigger an optional feature:

  • Google Drive Backup (Optional): If you connect your own Google Drive in Settings → Data & Backups, encrypted backup files are uploaded solely to your own Google Drive account's private app/backup space.
  • WhatsApp Share (Optional): Clicking Share on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp on your device with the invoice summary you chose to send.

#Data export, erasure & control

Because all records reside on your own device, you have immediate, unrestricted control at all times:

  • Export / Portability: Export all data as JSON, CSV, Excel, GSTR-1/3B JSON, or encrypted .fgsb archives from Settings → Data & Backups.
  • Erasure: Delete individual records inside the app or clear site storage / use Reset in Settings to permanently erase local data from your device.
  • Creator & Maintainer: Created by Abhishek Agrahari for Indian businesses.
