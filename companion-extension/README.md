# ⚡ Bharat GST Portal Auto-Fill Bridge (`v5.0.0` · Zero-GSP Companion Extension)

Auto-fill **NIC E-Way Bill (`ewaybillgst.gov.in`)** and **GST Portal (`gst.gov.in`)** forms in 1 click directly from **Bharat GST Free Billing** — with **₹0 GSP subscription cost** and **100% local browser storage**.

## Install in 30 Seconds (Chrome / Edge / Brave)
1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`).
2. Turn ON **Developer mode** (top-right toggle).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select this `companion-extension/` folder.
4. In **Bharat GST Free Billing → Reports → Enterprise AI & POS Hub → ITR-3/4, AIS & Portal Bridge**, click **⚡ Push Bill to Portal Extension**, then open `ewaybillgst.gov.in` or `gst.gov.in` and click the green floating **⚡ Auto-Fill Bill** button!
